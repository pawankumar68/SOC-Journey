## Task 1 — The Open Door
Cybersecurity professionals are in high demand across various
sectors — healthcare, finance, government, education and more.
Many roles in cybersecurity are still unfilled, creating huge
opportunities for people entering the field.

## Task 2 — What It Pays
Cybersecurity is one of the highest paying domains in tech.
The best part — no specific degree, license or certificate is
mandatory to enter this field. Anyone with the right skills
and dedication can build a career in cybersecurity.

## Task 3 — The Bigger Picture
When attackers strike, the impact can be massive:
- Hospitals delay appointments
- Banks freeze accounts
- Personal data gets stolen
- Critical infrastructure goes down

The people who stop these attacks are not lucky —
they are prepared. Cybersecurity professionals prepare
and protect systems before attacks happen.

## Task 4 — Find Your Place
Cybersecurity is not one job — it has 50+ different roles.
The right path depends on your interests:
- Do you like attacking systems? → Offensive Security / Pentesting
- Do you like defending? → SOC Analyst / Blue Team
- Do you like investigation? → Incident Response / Forensics
- Do you like code? → Security Engineering / AppSec

## Task 5 — Your Starting Point
Cybersecurity is one of the fastest growing fields in the world.
Every new technology — cloud, AI, IoT — expands it further.
The best time to start is now. Skills built today will be
relevant for years to come.
