# Junior Security Analyst Intro

## What This Room Is About
This room introduces the role of a Junior SOC Analyst and explains
why defenders are essential  to an organization's security.

## Key Things I Learned
- A SOC Analyst is the first line of defense for any organization
- Defenders are just as important as attackers in cybersecurity
- SOC teams work 24/7 to monitor and protect systems

## SOC Analyst Roles
- **L1 Analyst** — Monitors alerts, triages incidents, escalates threats
- **L2 Analyst** — Deep investigation, threat hunting
- **L3 Analyst** — Malware analysis, detection rule creation
- **Senior SOC Analyst** — Leads investigations, mentors L1/L2
- **SOC Manager** — Manages the entire SOC team and operations

## Hands-On Practice
- Accessed a SIEM dashboard and monitored live alerts
- Identified critical malicious IP addresses flagged in the dashboard
- Investigated IPs using IP Hunter threat intelligence tool
- Confirmed IPs were malicious based on threat intel results
- Blocked the malicious IPs to stop potential threats

## How This Connects to Real SOC Work
This is exactly what an L1 SOC Analyst does daily —
monitor dashboards, investigate suspicious activity,
and take action before damage spreads.
